# 🤖 BizOrder

### AI-Powered Business Ordering & Inventory Management System

</div>

**BizOrder** is an e-commerce-style platform I'm currently developing for small businesses.

The goal is to bring **ordering, inventory management, business operations, and AI-powered functionality** together in one application.

### 🛒 Customer Experience

* Browse products
* Search products
* Place orders
* Manage orders

### 📊 Business Management

* Manage products
* Track inventory
* Process orders
* Business dashboard

### 🧠 AI Integration

I'm currently exploring how AI can be integrated into BizOrder to make the application more intelligent and useful for business owners.

> **The goal isn't just to build another CRUD application — it's to build a real business application with useful AI capabilities.**
